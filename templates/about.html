About FirstAid AI

FirstAid AI is a modern web-based first-aid assistance platform designed to provide accessible, structured, step-by-step emergency guidance during stressful medical situations.

During medical emergencies, seconds matter. FirstAid AI simplifies decision-making by offering high-visibility instructions, an AI-powered natural language classification engine to quickly identify emergencies from user descriptions, voice read-aloud support, and instant access to emergency response hotlines (112).

Key Features & Architecture

Emergency-First Interface

High-contrast layout designed for rapid navigation and low cognitive load during high-stress situations.

Step-by-Step Guidance

Clear, numbered action steps for 15+ common emergency categories with step-by-step pagination.

AI Intent Classification

Integrated Python NLP engine matching natural language user queries (e.g. "my friend is choking") to the exact emergency topic.

Voice Read-Aloud (TTS)

Browser Web Speech API integration reading emergency steps hands-free so rescuers can focus on the victim.

112 Emergency Access

One-touch mobile click-to-call functionality for 112 emergency services available globally on all pages.

Quick Emergency Mode

Ultra-large text overlay mode hiding secondary UI for extreme readability and fast single-thumb operation.

Mandatory Medical Safety Disclaimer

FirstAid AI is an educational emergency information reference system. It is NOT a substitute for professional medical care, diagnosis, paramedical response, or clinical judgment.

  • FirstAid AI does not diagnose medical diseases or clinical conditions.
  • FirstAid AI does not prescribe medications or calculate drug dosages.
  • FirstAid AI does not replace emergency paramedics, doctors, or hospital treatment.
  • In any critical or life-threatening situation, call 112 immediately.
